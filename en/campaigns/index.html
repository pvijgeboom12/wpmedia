Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Is your question not here? Ask it in a short call, or email info@waterproofmedia.com.

Plan an intro call
Who are these campaigns for?

For freelancers, organisations and brands that want more reach, leads or enquiries. Whether you are starting your first campaign or already advertising and want better results.

Which channels do you advertise on?

Meta, so Facebook and Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. Which channel fits depends on your goal and your audience. We choose together in the first step.

I already advertise. Can you improve my campaigns?

Yes. I first look at what is running, what works and what could be better. Then we adjust where the biggest opportunities are. You do not have to start over.

How does the hour card work?

We agree upfront how many hours I will spend. I invoice those hours via a prepaid hour card. You always know where you stand, and hours never add up unnoticed.

Can I really stop at any time?

Yes. If the collaboration does not work for you, you can stop. You are not tied to a long contract.

What does it cost?

That depends on your goal and how much support you need. In a short intro call we discuss what needs to happen. Then we agree the hours and the price upfront, so there are no surprises.

Is the media budget included in the hours?

No. The media budget is separate from my hours. It is the amount you pay Meta, TikTok or LinkedIn for the ads. You decide how high it is. I advise you on a sensible split.

How much media budget do I need?

That depends on your goal, your audience and the channel. A big budget is not always needed. For BooyaKa Festival, €422.87 in media brought 54 people into the room. We started small, watched what worked and adjusted every day. Read the case.

Do you create the ads yourself?

I help with angles, ad copy and creative variants to test. If more images or video are needed, I work with trusted freelancers for animation, design and TikTok content, among others.

What do you need from me to get started?

A clear goal, an idea of your audience and access to your ad accounts. You grant that access yourself, through partner access, so I never get your password. It also helps to have visual material and a page the ads can point to.

Does the campaign run in my own ad account?

Preferably, yes. That way the account, the audiences and all the data stay yours, even after we stop.

How do we measure whether it works?

Before we start, we define what success means, for example sign-ups, enquiries or sales, and make sure it can be measured. During the campaign I follow the results and tell you what works and what we change.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and I am honest about that. Nobody can promise upfront how people will respond. What I do is test different angles, follow the numbers and put more behind what works. That way you quickly learn what moves your audience.

Who will I be in contact with?

With me. You talk directly to the person doing the work. There is no account manager in between.

Want to talk it through first?

Tell me what you want to achieve. Then we’ll find the right approach together.
