Privacy notice

This notice explains what data Waterproofmedia uses, why we use it and what choices you have.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who is responsible?

The controller for this website and the processing of personal data is Waterproofmedia. Registered address: Johan van Hasseltkade 198, 1032 LP Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number: 74282735. Contact: info@waterproofmedia.com.

What data do we process?

If you use the contact form, we process the details you provide: name, email address, message and, if provided, organisation, website, phone number, topic and preferred start time. We also process your language choice, the page where you submit the form and technical information needed to handle your request and limit misuse.

If you use the website assistant, your message is processed to generate a reply. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information. The assistant uses an AI service from Anthropic. If you arrive through a campaign or referral, UTM tags, the referring website and landing page may be added to a contact request.

Why do we use your data?

We use your request to reply, contact you and prepare a possible introduction call or proposal. We use technical data to secure and maintain the website and investigate issues. The legal basis and retention period for each purpose must be reviewed and completed before publication.

Who do we share data with?

We use service providers for hosting, security, form processing, email and the website assistant. The current technical setup uses Cloudflare for the website and server functions, Make to route enquiries and Anthropic to generate AI assistant replies. The exact providers, processing agreements and any transfer outside the European Economic Area must be confirmed before publication.

The website code includes Google Analytics 4. The current consent settings and how visitors can make choices still need to be checked. Your privacy rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data. You may also object to processing or request restriction or portability where those rights apply. Email your request to info@waterproofmedia.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

We may update this notice when our website or practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Questions? Email info@waterproofmedia.com.
