Every campaign is a growth experiment.

A campaign is not an end point. It is a test that teaches you something. That is why we measure from day one and adjust while there is still budget left.

1

Measure

We get measurement right first: pixel, events and one registration page. Otherwise you never know what worked.

2

Test

We launch several variants and read the numbers every day. What does not work gets switched off.

3

Scale

Budget moves to what demonstrably works. What we learn goes into the next round.
